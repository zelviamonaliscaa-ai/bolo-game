Bolo · Ten Lights
About This Little Journey

Nine nights of strength.
Ten arrows of release.

Drag on the screen to aim, then release to shoot. Each mask is named below it; the gold dots above show how many hits remain. When a mask raises its shield, wait until its face appears again. Missing a shot does not end the game. Your strength returns gradually; hold “Recharge” to restore it faster.

“Easy mode” slows the masks and lowers their shields. Use Tab to select a target below the game, then press Enter to shoot. You can pause and continue at any time.

The Story and This Adaptation

Some Dussehra / Vijayadashami celebrations mark victory by burning an effigy of Ravana. In this game, Bolo joins a symbolic festival challenge inspired by that tradition.

The ten masks and their matching strengths are an original framework for reflection, rather than a fixed traditional interpretation of Ravana’s ten heads. When anger or fear arises, we practise making room for our feelings without letting them lead us. The three streams of light and rainbow leaf crown symbolise growth.

References: UCLA · Dussehra; Ramayana · Yuddha Kanda, Chapter 107 and Chapter 108. In the original epic, the heads grow back; the final divine arrow strikes the heart. This game’s individual mask challenges are an adaptation.
